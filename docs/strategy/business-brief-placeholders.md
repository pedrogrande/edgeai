| **Can you provide an overview of your business concept or idea? (What is the business about, and what problem does it solve?)** | Future's Edge is a trust infrastructure company for agentic AI. We provide the methodology that gets AI agents into production safely — not principles, not tools, but a repeatable, teachable process. **The problem:** 77% of organisations attempting to deploy agentic AI are stuck at pilot. 40%+ of projects will be cancelled by 2027 (Gartner). Only 24% of complex tasks are completed successfully by agents (Carnegie Mellon). The root cause isn't a technology gap — it's a methodology gap. The market offers principles, patterns, and tools, but no one provides a systematic process for designing trustworthy agent workflows. Organisations don't know which tasks agents should handle, where human judgment is essential, how to set boundaries, or how to verify that agent outputs are trustworthy enough to put their name on. **The solution:** The Complementarity-Driven Agentic Workflow Design Process (CAWDP) — a methodology that decomposes any workflow into human, agent, and system tasks based on complementarity analysis, designs authority boundaries tied to failure modes, and progressively earns trust through structured verification. CAWDP's core principle: "The agent prepares judgment; the human makes judgment." We're not building agents. We're building the methodology that makes agents trustworthy in production. |

They're being bombarded by AI vendors selling tools, and they need someone who sells clarity. |
