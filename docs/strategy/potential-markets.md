## What "Business Owner as Learner" Actually Means

Before we pick the target, I want to name what this constraint does to your design:

- **They can give you half a day, maybe.** Not a 3-day bootcamp. Not a 12-week course first up. Half a day, once. If they see value, they come back.
- **They need to see their own problem on the table in the first 30 minutes.** Not "AI is transforming business." Not "the market will be worth $52B by 2030." Their problem. Their inbox. Their quoting bottleneck. Their document review pile.
- **They decide by doing, not by hearing.** They're business owners, not students. They trust what they can touch.
- **They need peers in the room.** Isolation is one of the heaviest things small business owners carry. A workshop that puts them with other owners who have the same problem is already valuable before you teach anything.

That last point is actually a market entry insight: **the workshop IS the community seed.** Every person in that room is a future community member, case study contributor, and word-of-mouth engine.

## So: Where Do You Point the 77% Challenge?

**Primary: Small law firms and accounting practices, 3-15 people, in Melbourne and Sydney.**

Here's what that looks like concretely:

**The Offer:** *"Free 30-minute complementarity analysis of your document review workflow. We'll map which tasks AI should handle, which need human judgment, and where the boundary should be. No tools to buy. No vendor pitch. Just clarity."*

**Why this works for this audience:**
- They're being sold "AI document review" tools weekly. They're suspicious but curious.
- A free, no-pitch analysis that gives them a clear boundary map? That's not a sales call — that's a practice management consultation.
- The output is a one-page complementarity map they can actually use — it IS the trust artifact.

**What happens after:**
- They see the map. They see the boundary. They say "I need this for my whole practice."
- You say: "I'm running a half-day workshop for 12 firm owners. Same methodology. Bring your actual workflows. You'll walk out with a complete boundary map for your practice."
- Workshop → case study → law society presentation → "77% of firms are stuck at pilot with AI. Here's why, and here's the methodology that gets you to production."

**The half-day workshop design for this audience:**
- **First 30 min:** They bring one real workflow (document review, client onboarding, compliance reporting). They map it themselves. You facilitate.
- **Next 60 min:** You introduce the 5-class taxonomy and complementarity allocation — but you call it "what should AI do, what should YOU do, and how do you know?" Plain language.
- **Next 60 min:** They apply it to their own workflow. They leave with a real boundary map.
- **Final 30 min:** "Here's the full methodology. Here's how to go deeper. Here's what certified looks like."

They walk out having DONE something. Not heard something.
