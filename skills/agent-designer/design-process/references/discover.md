# Phase 1: DISCOVER

## Goal
Understand what the agent is for, who uses it, and what kind of cognitive work it does.

## Questions to Explore

Ask these naturally — not as a questionnaire. Let the user's answers lead to the next question.

### 1.1 Purpose
- What is the agent supposed to do? (1–3 sentences)
- What problem does it solve?
- What does a successful outcome look like?

### 1.2 Users
- Who will use this agent?
  - Non-technical end users
  - Semi-technical users
  - Developers/engineers
  - Other agents/automated systems
  - Internal team members

### 1.3 Cognitive Mode
Classify by primary cognitive operation:
- **Extractor**: Gathers/collects information. Never judges.
- **Measurer**: Quantifies/measures against criteria. Never interprets.
- **Assessor**: Evaluates against standards. Human makes the final call.
- **Generator**: Produces content/drafts. Must be verified.
- **Aggregator**: Combines/synthesizes. Must not add beyond inputs.

## Tips
- If the user is non-technical, avoid jargon. Use analogies (load `non-technical-explanations` skill).
- You don't need every template answer up front — get enough to proceed to SCOPE.
- Pay attention to what the user *doesn't* say — those gaps often surface important constraints later.
